## Small equations

Energy and mass: $E = mc^2$.

A familiar derivative: $\frac{d}{dx}x^n = nx^{n-1}$.

The Gaussian: $f(x) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}e^{-x^2/2}$.

中文笔记也可以混排：$a^2 + b^2 = c^2$。
